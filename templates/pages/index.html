{% trans "What conditions does a urology surgeon treat?" %}

{% trans "The specialty includes treatment of kidney stones, prostate enlargement, urinary tract infections, kidney and bladder tumors, as well as infertility issues and urinary disorders in men and women." %}

{% trans "When should I visit a urology surgeon?" %}

{% trans "It is recommended to consult the doctor if you experience persistent pain in the kidneys or bladder, difficulty or pain during urination, blood in urine, recurring urinary tract infections, or fertility problems." %}

{% trans "Do all urinary tract problems require surgery?" %}

{% trans "Not necessarily. Some cases can be treated with medications or minor procedures like endoscopy, while some conditions require advanced surgical intervention depending on the diagnosis." %}

{% trans "Can kidney stones be treated without surgery?" %}

{% trans "In many cases, stones can be broken down using shock wave therapy or endoscopic procedures without the need for open surgery." %}

{% trans "Does an enlarged prostate mean cancer?" %}

{% trans "Benign prostate enlargement is common with age and does not necessarily indicate cancer, but it is important to consult the doctor for proper testing." %}
